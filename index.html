Ghostwriting Books

Your book, in your voice, on a spine a reader can use.

Most expert books fail before the first chapter: they are a life told in order when the reader wanted a method, or a method with no life in it.

The first thing I do is choose which book yours is, with you. Then the spine: one claim, a chapter map that says what each chapter proves, and one sample chapter in your voice. You approve the spine before the rest is written.
